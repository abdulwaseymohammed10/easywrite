EasyWrite

Free spelling and grammar help for your Windows laptop. Works in Word and email. Private — nothing you write leaves your computer.

⬇  Download EasyWrite

For Windows 10 and 11 · Free · No account needed

How to install

  1. Click the green button above.
    If the browser says the file “isn't commonly downloaded”, click Keep.
  2. Open the downloaded file “EasyWrite”.
    If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This is normal for free apps.
  3. Click the green “Start setup” button, then wait.
    It downloads what it needs one time (10–30 minutes). Keep the laptop plugged in. When it says “All done!”, click “Open EasyWrite”. That's it — it starts by itself every day from now on.

How to use it

  • In Word and email: just type. Mistakes get a coloured line — click the word and pick the fix.
  • Fix everything at once in Word: click the green “Fix all mistakes” button at the bottom-right of the page.
  • Anywhere else: highlight some text and press Ctrl + Alt + G.
  • Make it professional, friendly or shorter: open EasyWrite from the desktop, paste your text and press a button.

EasyWrite is free. It uses free and open-source tools: LanguageTool (spelling & grammar), Eclipse Temurin (Java), llama.cpp, and Meta's Llama 3.2. Built with Llama. Licences and notices.
